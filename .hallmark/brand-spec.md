# KeyPharm brand sources

Verified 23 November 2026 against the [KeyPharm website](https://www.keypharm.com/).

- The official [KeyPharm Laboratories logo](https://www.keypharm.com/images/keypharm-logo.png) contains green `#064D19` and ink `#141215`. The app uses the original PNG and crops its outer whitespace in CSS.
- The Android launcher uses the official [KeyPharm site icon](https://www.keypharm.com/images/favicon.png).
- [The general stylesheet](https://www.keypharm.com/styles/algemeen.css) contains teal `#00654B`. [The interface stylesheet](https://www.keypharm.com/styles/ux2014.css) contains deep green `#013B10`. The app uses teal for focus and active detail and deep green for press and hover states.
- Pale green and neutral surfaces are app adaptations. The UI retains its local system font for offline operation and preserves distinct match and exception colors.
